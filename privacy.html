1. Who we are

The McMaster Bachelor of Health Sciences Society (“BHSS”, “we”, “us”) is a student society at McMaster University in Hamilton, Ontario. We run this website (bhssmcmaster.com) to share information about our committees, resources, events and store, and to give BHSS-affiliated groups (BAGs) and committees a place to submit requests.

This website is run by BHSS student volunteers. It is separate from McMaster University’s own websites and systems, and McMaster’s own privacy notices apply to those. If you have a question about this policy or your information, contact us at bhss@mcmaster.ca.

2. What information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the subject you pick, and your message.
  • Student Groups portal: the group name and type tied to your authorization code, the code itself, and everything you enter into a form (for example room bookings, storage requests, media requests, reimbursements, and Zeffy listing requests). This can include names, contact details, event details and amounts of money.
  • Uploaded files: PDF files you attach to forms, such as receipts and budget spreadsheets. Receipts can contain personal and financial details, so please black out anything that is not needed (such as full card numbers).
  • Store and ticket purchases: these are processed by Zeffy (see section 4). We receive your name, email address, what you bought, the amount, and the order status. We never receive your card number. Some older orders also include a McMaster ID or notes you entered at checkout.

Information about BHSS members

The Committees pages show the names, roles and photos of BHSS members. Members can ask us at any time to update or remove their listing. BHSS volunteers who manage the website sign in with an email address and password; we store the account details needed to let them in.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: see “Analytics” in section 4. Our web host and the third-party services we use also receive technical details your browser sends automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you request.
  • Browser storage: when you sign in to the Student Groups portal, your group name, group type and authorization code are kept in your browser’s session storage so you stay signed in. They are cleared when you sign out or close the tab. We do not use advertising cookies.

3. How we use your information

  • to read and reply to your messages;
  • to run the Student Groups portal and act on requests, such as approving room bookings, processing reimbursements, and listing items in the store;
  • to confirm and manage store and ticket orders and answer questions about them;
  • to keep a record of requests and finances for BHSS’s accounting and reporting;
  • to understand how the website is used so we can improve it;
  • to protect the website and our members from misuse, and to meet legal or McMaster University policy requirements.

We use your information only for the purposes you would reasonably expect from the context in which you gave it. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

Within BHSS, information is available only to the volunteers who need it for their role (for example, the Financial Committee sees reimbursement receipts). Admin access requires a signed-in account.

We use these service providers, which handle information on our behalf or receive it when you use the site:

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage (including private storage for uploaded receipts) and admin sign-in.
  • Zeffy processes store and ticket payments. Zeffy’s own terms and privacy policy apply to anything you enter on its payment forms.
  • Google provides the fonts used on this site, which your browser downloads directly from Google, and our analytics (below).
  • SnapWidget displays our Instagram feed on the home page. Instagram and SnapWidget may set their own cookies.
  • Notion is where we maintain the public events and academic calendar. We pull public event details from it; no visitor information is sent to Notion.
  • Our web hosting provider serves the website and may keep standard server logs.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It collects information such as your approximate location, device, browser and the pages you view, using cookies or similar technologies. We do not use it to identify you personally. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or where needed to follow McMaster University policy or to protect the safety of a person.

Storage outside Canada. Some of these providers store or process data on servers outside Canada, where privacy laws may differ from Canadian law and where governments may be able to access the data under their own laws.

5. How long we keep it

  • Contact messages: for as long as needed to respond and keep reasonable records, then deleted.
  • Reimbursement receipts and other uploaded files: kept through the BHSS fiscal year (May 1 to April 30) in which they were submitted, so that finance can reconcile and, if needed, audit. They are then deleted. Finance may keep them longer where an accounting or legal reason requires it.
  • Form submissions and orders: kept as long as needed to complete the request and keep BHSS’s financial and event records.
  • Authorization codes: issued for a school year; old batches are deactivated when new ones are issued.

6. How we protect it

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Admin tools require a signed-in account, database access rules limit what the public website can read, and uploaded receipts are stored in a private bucket that the public cannot open. No online service is perfectly secure, so please share only what a request needs, and never share your group’s authorization code outside your group.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to our using it, subject to legal and record-keeping limits. Email bhss@mcmaster.ca and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Children

This website is meant for university students and the wider McMaster community. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will say so on the website.
