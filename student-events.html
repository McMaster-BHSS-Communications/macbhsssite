Student Events

Plan Your Event

Want to plan an event for your group? Here's everything you need to plan an event with the BHSS — risk management, room bookings, reimbursements, and accessibility guidelines. Please ensure that all information you provide is accurate and complete. Incomplete submissions may be delayed or rejected.

EOHSS Forms

In order to run any event affiliated with McMaster University, you must first submit your event for approval by Environmental & Occupational Health Support Services (EOHSS). As a BHSS-Affiliated Group, the BHSS will submit this form on your behalf. You should fill in this form for ANY event you are hosting, including low-risk events (e.g., official club meetings) and events off-campus.

Request a Room Booking

Request a space on campus for your event. You may be responsible for booking your own space for events. In order to ensure that you receive approval and have enough time to book a room on campus, we recommend starting this process at least 10 business days in advance of your planned date.

Request a Reimbursement

Submit a reimbursement request for approved expenses.

Request Storage Space

Request storage space for BAG or committee materials. Only available to signed-in BAGs and committees.

Event Guidelines and Accessibility

Everything to consider when planning an accessible, safe, and successful event with the BHSS.

Planning Ahead

[Placeholder] Start planning early — most approvals (room bookings, EOHSS review) take at least two weeks. Consider the size of your event, the space required, and any equipment or vendors involved.

Accessibility

[Placeholder] Choose accessible venues wherever possible, and consider:

  • Wheelchair-accessible entrances, washrooms, and seating
  • Dietary accommodations for food-based events
  • Sensory-friendly options (lighting, sound levels)
  • Clear, advance communication about event accessibility

Safety

[Placeholder] Identify potential risks ahead of time and outline them in your EOHSS submission — this includes physical activity, food handling, off-campus travel, and large crowds.

Questions?

Reach out through the Contact page if you're unsure whether your event needs additional approval.

Brand and Name Use Guidelines

How to properly represent the BHSS name, logo, and brand when planning an event or making materials.

Using the BHSS Name

[Placeholder] Always refer to the society by its full name ("Bachelor of Health Sciences Society" or "BHSS") on official materials. Only BAGs and committees in good standing may describe an event as "BHSS-affiliated" or "in partnership with the BHSS."

Logo Use

[Placeholder] Use only the official BHSS logo files provided by the Communications Committee — don't recreate, recolour, or distort the logo. Maintain clear space around it and avoid placing it on busy backgrounds.

Approval for External Materials

[Placeholder] Any posters, merchandise, or social media posts that use the BHSS name or logo and are intended for distribution outside your group should be reviewed by the Communications Committee before publishing.

Questions?

Reach out to the Communications Committee through the Contact page for logo files or brand approval.
