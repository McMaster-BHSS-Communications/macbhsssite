1. Agreeing to these terms

By using the McMaster Bachelor of Health Sciences Society (“BHSS”) website at bhssmcmaster.com, you agree to these Terms of Use and to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

2. About this website

BHSS is a student society at McMaster University. This website is run by BHSS student volunteers to share information and to give affiliated groups and committees a place to submit requests. It is not an official McMaster University website, and nothing on it is a statement or policy of McMaster University unless it says so.

3. Using the website

You may browse the website and use its features for lawful, personal or student-group purposes. You agree not to:

  • break, probe, overload or try to gain unauthorized access to the website, its admin tools or its databases;
  • use automated tools to scrape or copy the site in a way that harms its performance;
  • submit false, misleading, abusive, hateful or unlawful content, or content that violates someone else’s rights;
  • impersonate another person, group or committee;
  • upload files that contain malware or that you have no right to share.

You must also follow the McMaster University policies that apply to students, such as the Code of Student Rights & Responsibilities.

4. Student Groups portal and authorization codes

  • The portal is for representatives of BHSS committees and BHSS-affiliated groups. Your authorization code belongs to your group. Keep it confidential and do not share it outside your group.
  • You are responsible for what is submitted using your group’s code, and for making sure your answers and uploads are accurate and that you are allowed to submit them.
  • A submission is a request, not an approval. Room bookings, storage, media and collaboration requests, reimbursements and store listings are reviewed by BHSS and may be changed, delayed or declined, including under BHSS or McMaster policies.
  • Uploads are limited to PDF files of 20 MB or less.
  • We may deactivate or reassign a code at any time, for example when a new batch is issued or if we believe it is being misused.

5. Store and ticket purchases

Purchases are made through Zeffy, and Zeffy’s terms apply to the payment itself. Prices, items, dates and availability are shown on each store or ticket listing and may change. Refund and pick-up details are set out on the listing or in the confirmation you receive. For help with an order, email bhss@mcmaster.ca and include your order reference.

6. Messages and emergencies

Messages sent through the website are read by BHSS volunteers and are not monitored around the clock. Do not use the website to report an emergency. In an emergency, call 911 or McMaster University Security Services.

7. Content and ownership

The BHSS name, logo, committee artwork, text and other content on this site belong to BHSS or are used with permission. Logos and descriptions of BHSS-affiliated groups belong to those groups. You may view and share pages for personal, non-commercial use. For any other use, such as copying our artwork or branding, please ask us first at bhss@mcmaster.ca.

If you send us content (for example a message or a form submission), you confirm that you may share it, and you allow BHSS to use it to handle your request and to keep records, as described in the Privacy Policy.

8. Other websites

The website links to and embeds third-party services such as Zeffy, Instagram and Notion. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content, availability or privacy practices. Your use of them is covered by their own terms.

9. Information on the site

We work to keep the website accurate, but the resources, calendar, dates, committee information and other content are provided “as is” and may be out of date or incomplete. They are not academic, medical, legal or financial advice. For anything that affects your studies, such as deadlines, program requirements and policies, check with the official McMaster University source.

10. Limits on our responsibility

To the fullest extent the law allows, BHSS and its volunteers are not liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential loss that comes from using, or not being able to use, the website, including outages, errors in content, or the actions of third-party services. Nothing in these terms limits any right or liability that cannot be limited by law.

11. Changes to the website and these terms

We may change, suspend or remove any part of the website, and we may update these terms from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when they last changed. Continuing to use the website after a change means you accept the updated terms.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there. Any dispute will be handled by the courts of Ontario.

13. Contact us

Questions about these terms? Email bhss@mcmaster.ca or write to the McMaster Bachelor of Health Sciences Society, MDCL 3308, McMaster University, 1280 Main Street West, Hamilton, ON L8S 4K1.
