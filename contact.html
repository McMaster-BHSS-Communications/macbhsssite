Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to some of the questions we hear most often.

How do I join a BHSS committee?
Committee member applications typically open at the start of the academic year. Visit the Committees page to see which teams are recruiting and how to apply.
How do BHSS elections work?
Most coordinator and representative positions are filled through student elections run by the Logistics & Elections Committee, including nominations, campaigning, and voting. Reach out to bhssele@mcmaster.ca with questions about an upcoming election.
Where can I find academic resources?
Check the Resources page for study guides and program resources, or the "Academic Resources" link in the site navigation for the shared drive maintained by the Academics Committee.
What are BAGs (BHSS-Affiliated Groups)?
BAGs are BHSS-supported, student-run initiatives that offer additional ways to get involved. See the BAGs page for the current list of groups.
How do I reach a specific committee?
Every committee page lists its coordinators and a dedicated contact email. Find the right one on the Committees page, or use the "Who to Contact" section above.
I'm having a technical issue with the website — who do I tell?
Email the Communications Committee at bhsscom@mcmaster.ca, or use the form above with "Technical Issue" as the subject.
